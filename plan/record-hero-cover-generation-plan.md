# The Record Hero Cover Generation Plan

Date: 2026-06-30

Goal: create dedicated hero cover images for the 10 specified Record posts.

Output rules:
- Save each final image as `public/blog/<slug>/cover.png`.
- Use one consistent landscape ratio for all covers: `1672x941`.
- Keep the same fine-press / restrained editorial engraving language used for the article illustrations.
- Use restrained color only; no caption text, labels, watermarks, or readable typography inside the image.

## Targets

1. `anatomy-of-a-modern-coup` -> `public/blog/anatomy-of-a-modern-coup/cover.png`
   - A presidential palace at dusk seen wide: a colonnaded facade, an armored vehicle stopped at the gates, a cluster of soldiers in silhouette, a national flag hanging still at half-mast.

2. `what-makes-a-constitution-last` -> `public/blog/what-makes-a-constitution-last/cover.png`
   - A long archival shelf of bound constitutions receding into shadow; one ancient, cracked volume stands open and lit in the foreground while newer, thinner volumes lean beside it.

3. `the-quiet-power-of-the-second-chamber` -> `public/blog/the-quiet-power-of-the-second-chamber/cover.png`
   - A grand, near-empty upper-house chamber: two tiers of curved benches, a raised rostrum, dust-light from high windows; power at rest.

4. `term-limits-and-the-men-who-broke-them` -> `public/blog/term-limits-and-the-men-who-broke-them/cover.png`
   - A monumental clock face with its hands being wound backward by an unseen hand, set against a row of identical official portraits stretching too far down a corridor.

5. `how-democracies-measure-themselves` -> `public/blog/how-democracies-measure-themselves/cover.png`
   - A scholar's table of measuring instruments: brass calipers, a balance scale, a sextant, over a spread map of the world ruled into a faint grid of scores.

6. `the-worlds-last-absolute-monarchies` -> `public/blog/the-worlds-last-absolute-monarchies/cover.png`
   - A vast throne room seen wide: a single ornate throne on a dais beneath a canopy, long shadows across a marble floor, a crown resting alone on a cushion.

7. `why-the-voting-system-is-the-whole-game` -> `public/blog/why-the-voting-system-is-the-whole-game/cover.png`
   - A wide polling hall: rows of ballot boxes and curtained booths, a few figures voting, a tallying chalkboard half-filled at the side.

8. `backsliding-without-tanks` -> `public/blog/backsliding-without-tanks/cover.png`
   - A courthouse, a printing press, and a ballot box standing together in a quiet square as one long shadow slowly lengthens across all three. Calm, faintly ominous.

9. `who-actually-runs-a-country` -> `public/blog/who-actually-runs-a-country/cover.png`
   - Two desks in one wide hall: a ceremonial one with a sash and seal under a portrait, and a working one buried in papers and telephones; the second clearly where the work happens.

10. `governing-the-very-small` -> `public/blog/governing-the-very-small/cover.png`
    - A tiny island state from a slight elevation: a single small parliament building, a harbor, a flag; modest scale holding a full set of real institutions.
