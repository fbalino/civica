# Civica Atlas brand-confusion landscape

**Task:** BRD-001
**Research date:** 2026-07-23
**Scope:** “Civica Atlas,” “Civica,” close variants, and adjacent
“Atlas + governance” names
**Posture:** Neutral factual review; not legal advice or trademark clearance

## Executive finding

“Civica Atlas” currently has a distinct exact-phrase search footprint: the
project’s website, status page, repository, preview, and one independent blog
mention dominate the exact-name results reviewed on 2026-07-23. No reviewed
result showed a third party actively using the exact two-word name for a
governance-data product, and no documented incident of actual user confusion
was found.

The shared word **Civica**, however, is already prominent in several adjacent
contexts. Most importantly, Civica UK Limited is an active, long-established
public-sector software and data company. The official UK register shows that
company owns a registered **CIVICA** word mark covering software,
computerised information retrieval and data processing, training and
education, and software/computer-system services. Its public materials
describe government, data, analytics, research, community, and
decision-support offerings. Those facts create meaningful similarity of name,
sector, audience, and digital-service channels even though the products,
business models, and institutional purposes are not identical.

Other active uses include CIVICA, the European University of Social Sciences;
Data Cívica; Project Civica; and Civica Rx. Adjacent “Atlas + governance”
names include AtlasGov and the Urban Governance Atlas. They vary materially in
sector and geography, but collectively reduce the distinctiveness of the
individual words.

This landscape supports one operational conclusion only: **professional
confusion/trademark review is required before broad launch or a final keep-name
decision.** It does not establish infringement, registrability, priority,
coexistence, clearance, or a duty to rename.

## Project use being compared

Civica Atlas describes itself as a provenance-first comparative reference to
how countries are governed. Current and planned surfaces include:

- public country and institutional profiles;
- source-linked governance data and downloadable research artifacts;
- APIs, documentation, citation, and teaching/research assets;
- a non-commercial current posture, with code licensing still unresolved; and
- intended use by researchers, educators, data practitioners, journalists,
  civil-society organizations, students, and general readers.

The comparison below uses that real product posture. It does not assume a paid
service, an unapproved geography, or a future offering not present in the
master checklist.

## Official mark and company observations

The separate BRD-002 registry snapshot archives the official records and
limitations. The findings most relevant to this landscape are:

1. UKIPO record **UK00002285308** is a registered word mark for **CIVICA**,
   owned by Civica UK Limited, filed 13 November 2001, with renewal shown to
   13 November 2031. It covers Nice classes 9, 16, 35, 41, and 42. The listed
   terms include computer software, downloadable and printed publications,
   computerised information retrieval and data processing, training and
   education, computer-system/software services, and software licensing.
2. UKIPO’s owner search showed 14 marks owned by Civica UK Limited, including
   registered `CIVICA`, `civica digital`, `CIVICAPAY`, `CIVICAPAYMENTS`,
   `CIVICA ESTORE`, `CIVICA PAYMENTS`, and `CIVICA ideas into action`.
3. WIPO Madrid Monitor record **1560925** showed `CIVICA ideas into action`
   active on 2026-07-23, held by Civica UK Limited in classes 9, 35, 38, 41,
   42, and 45, with designations including Australia, Canada, Ireland, New
   Zealand, Singapore, and the United States.
4. UK Companies House record **01628868** showed CIVICA UK LIMITED active,
   incorporated 14 April 1982, with “other information technology service
   activities” as its stated nature of business.

These are registry observations. Class overlap and registration status are
inputs for counsel; they are not findings that the project infringes or cannot
use its name.

## Domains, social identity, and search

### Domains

- `civicaatlas.org` is registered and currently used by this project. The
  captured Public Interest Registry RDAP record showed registration on
  16 April 2026 and expiry on 16 April 2027.
- `civica.com` is registered and used by the incumbent company. The captured
  Verisign RDAP record showed registration on 18 December 1998 and expiry on
  18 December 2028.
- `civica.org` and `civica.ai` were registered in the dated RDAP checks, but
  public registrant identity was not established and no affiliation was
  inferred.
- Domain registration is not a trademark right or clearance result.

### Social identity

- LinkedIn’s prominent `/company/civica` identity belonged to the incumbent
  software company and showed roughly 289,000 followers in the dated search
  result.
- CIVICA, the European University, had separate LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram,
  research-portal, and X identities.
- No claim is made that all desired `Civica Atlas` handles are available or
  reserved. Availability and platform-policy checks are still needed before a
  launch or migration decision.

### Search-result behavior

The following contact-free searches were run through Firecrawl on 2026-07-23:

- exact and broad: `"Civica Atlas"`, `Civica`, `Civica Atlas third party`;
- sector overlap: `Civica governance data`, `Civica data platform`,
  `Civica civic tech`, `Civica Atlas governance`;
- organizations/social: `Civica organizations`, `Civica social`,
  `CIVICA research`, `Data Cívica`, `Project Civica`;
- registry leads: `Civica Atlas trademark`, `CIVICA trademark Civica UK
  Limited`, and record-number follow-ups.

Observed result patterns:

- Exact `Civica Atlas` results were led by this project’s own live and preview
  surfaces. The only independent exact-name reference found was a short blog
  mention describing the project.
- `Civica` results were dominated by the incumbent software company and, in
  research/education contexts, the European university alliance.
- `Civica Atlas governance` returned this project first but also interleaved
  AtlasGov, incumbent Civica government/software pages, and other governance
  atlases.
- Several exact-phrase false positives were linguistic or bibliographic
  combinations, such as Italian “educazione civica” books from publisher
  Atlas and the species name *Brigittea civica* in an arachnid atlas.

Search order is volatile and personalized. It is evidence of current
discoverability and ambiguity, not legal priority.

## Actual-confusion indicators

### Observed

- No reviewed search result attributed Civica Atlas content to Civica UK
  Limited or to the European university alliance.
- No complaint, misdirected message, reported affiliation mistake, or other
  documented actual-confusion incident was found in the checked project
  evidence.
- One independent blog result used the exact project name and linked/described
  the project rather than a namesake.

### Potential indicators, not actual incidents

- The unqualified word `Civica` routes users toward established organizations,
  especially the incumbent software company.
- The incumbent and the project both use government, data, research,
  education, portal, and software/API language.
- The European university uses `CIVICA` in social-science research and
  education, including a research portal and data-science programming.
- Search results for “Civica Atlas governance” mix this project with other
  Civica and governance/Atlas offerings.

Absence of a found incident is not evidence that confusion cannot occur. The
project is young, the search was bounded, and no survey, support-log study,
market interview, or counsel-led confusion analysis was performed.

## Practical risk map

| Dimension | Evidence-based observation | What is not concluded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verbal similarity | Exact use of `CIVICA` within `Civica Atlas`; registered exact word mark exists in the UK | Whether `Atlas` is legally sufficient to distinguish the full name |
| Services | Software, data retrieval/processing, portals, education, and research-adjacent services overlap in part | Whether the parties’ goods/services are legally related in any jurisdiction |
| Audiences | Government/public-sector, education, research, and data users overlap | Whether consumers are likely to be confused |
| Channels | Websites, software, portals, publications, social accounts, and search channels overlap | Whether actual confusion has occurred |
| Geography | Project is web-accessible globally; incumbent operates internationally; WIPO designations include the US and other English-language markets | The project’s legally relevant territory, first-use rights, or filing strategy |
| Commercial posture | Civica Atlas currently states a non-commercial posture; incumbent is commercial | Whether non-commercial use changes liability, defenses, or registration options |

## Required next decision

BRD-003 should give counsel this report, the BRD-002 record snapshot, the
BRD-004 decision rubric, the intended launch/service/geography facts, current
visual/verbal marks, domains, and migration inventory. Until counsel and the
owner record a disposition:

- do not call the name cleared, available, protected, non-infringing, or safe;
- do not file a mark, contact another rights holder, buy replacement domains,
  or launch a rename campaign without owner authority;
- preserve the reversible keep/rename path under BRD-006; and
- avoid broad launch claims that imply affiliation with any namesake.
