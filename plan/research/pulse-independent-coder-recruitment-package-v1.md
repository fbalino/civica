# Pulse independent-coder recruitment package v1

Status: prepared, not approved, no contact made

Contract: `pulse-independent-coder-recruitment/v1`

Frozen evaluation protocol: `pulse-evaluation-sampling-frame/v1`

This package prepares recruitment for independent human coding of the frozen Pulse evaluation sets. It does not authorize contact, spending, or a gold release. Recruitment may begin only after the owner approves the budget and terms and the master plan reaches its external-contact gate.

## Roles and separation

### Independent coder

Minimum qualification follows either of these paths:

- a completed master's degree or current doctoral study in comparative politics, political science, international relations, public law, human rights, conflict studies, or a closely related field; or
- a bachelor's degree plus at least two years of documented professional country research, political-event coding, human-rights documentation, or comparable evidence review.

Every coder must show experience reading political or institutional evidence, explain uncertain classifications in writing, and work only in languages they can assess professionally. A translated item may support coding, but it does not establish original-language competence.

### Independent adjudicator

An adjudicator needs the coder qualifications plus senior experience in comparative institutional research, political-event data, content analysis, or research-method design. A doctorate is preferred. Equivalent publication, dataset, or professional evidence-review experience may substitute. The adjudicator cannot have coded the packet being resolved.

### Study administrator

The administrator issues access, monitors completion, answers procedural questions, and handles payment records. While coding is active, this role sees status rather than label content. The Civica owner may administer logistics but may not code, adjudicate, supply an answer key, or resolve a substantive coding question.

### Panel structure

The working plan uses four coders and two adjudicators. Each packet goes to two coders. Pairings should cross institutions or immediate research teams where feasible. No participant may act as both coder and adjudicator for the same packet. A production contributor, prior Civica reviewer, prompt designer, or anyone who has seen production labels is ineligible for coding or adjudication.

## Independence and conflicts

Before access, each participant must disclose:

- current or recent work for Civica, a data supplier, a government, political party, campaign, advocacy organization, or security actor relevant to assigned cases;
- funding, employment, close collaboration, or a direct supervisory relationship that could affect independent judgment;
- prior access to Civica production labels, owner approvals, model votes, unpublished evaluation results, or another coder's work; and
- countries, organizations, or events for which recusal is appropriate.

The methods lead records a recusal or a documented no-conflict decision before assignment. Coders may discuss instructions and software problems through a shared question channel, but not packet content. Clarifications go to every participant without labels or hints about the preferred answer. The owner cannot answer substantive label questions.

The first qualified human timing pilot must replace the assumed minutes before the full assignment is released.

## Compensation options

All training, qualification, coding, requested clarification, unusable assigned packets, and adjudication time is paid. Piece rates are unsuitable because they reward speed on the packets that most need caution. Rates are fixed before labels are visible and do not depend on agreement with Civica or on a preferred outcome.

| Plan | Coder / adjudicator / administrator hourly rates | Ten-percent budget contingency | Estimated budget |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Low | $25 / $40 / $35 | included | **$16,751** |
| Base | $40 / $65 / $50 | included | **$45,615** |
| High | $60 / $90 / $70 | included | **$104,801** |

These are USD planning ranges, not offers. Currency conversion, payment fees, employment classification, taxes, and any institutional overhead require separate treatment. The US Bureau of Labor Statistics reports master's-level entry and a May 2024 median of $67.01 per hour for political scientists; this is an upper professional-market anchor, not a contractor quote. Prolific recommends $12 per hour and enforces an $8 minimum for general research participants. That floor is too low for specialist political coding and is included only to prevent accidental crowd-work pricing.

The preferred option is the base plan with paid hourly time. If the budget cannot support it, reduce or stage the research question under a new preregistered design. Do not preserve the same claimed inference by cutting coder independence, adjudication, payment, or sample coverage after labels are known.

## Training and qualification

1. **Conflict and blindness check.** Record eligibility, recusal needs, language scope, and confirmation that the participant has not seen production labels.
2. **Codebook study.** Read `pulse-independent-coding/v1`, the ontology boundaries, observability rules, and the distinction between evidence absence and a true negative.
3. **Worked examples.** Complete the six descriptively named training examples with feedback on evidence use, uncertainty, abstention, multi-label facets, and exclusions.
4. **Workspace practice.** Save drafts, add candidate events, cite evidence, and lock practice records in the dedicated workspace. No production review screen is used.
5. **Held-out qualification.** Complete a separate qualification set whose key is created and adjudicated by qualified humans independent of Civica production. The owner, model output, and the 12-agent dry pilot cannot define the key.
6. **Mechanical gates.** Achieve 100% valid schema, evidence-reference integrity, required rationale completion, and blind-boundary compliance.
7. **Substantive gate.** Meet a preregistered threshold on packet outcome and event axes against the independent qualification key. Thresholds and the key must be frozen before applicants start.
8. **Adjudicator gate.** Pass the coder gates, then complete written disagreement resolutions reviewed by two qualified method leads who did not code those cases.
9. **Paid timing pilot.** Time at least 30 packets across all three frames and major evidence states. Replace the planning minutes before full assignment and obtain owner approval if the base budget changes by more than 15%.

Failure on a qualification attempt does not create evaluation data. One retraining attempt may be offered. A second failure ends eligibility for this study without changing the qualification threshold.

## Sourcing pools

No individual has been identified or contacted. Recruitment should use public calls or association administrators so the same role description reaches a broad pool.

| Pool | Use | Independence and coverage check |
|---|---|---|
| APSA Comparative Politics Section and adjacent organized sections | Comparative institutions, area studies, methods | Exclude Civica contributors and prior label viewers; avoid one-institution coder pairs |
| ECPR standing groups and research networks | European and cross-national scholars, including autocracy, conflict, rights, and methods | Recruit across different groups and institutions rather than one existing team |
| IAPSS | Global graduate and early-career political scientists | Apply the same qualification gate; student status alone is not sufficient |
| Regional and national associations found through the MPSA directory | Language and region coverage beyond US and Western European networks | Track region and professional language; do not infer either from nationality |
| University doctoral programs, research centers, and public academic job boards | Paid research assistants and postdoctoral researchers | Use a public call through program administrators; no private supervisor nomination without conflict disclosure |
| Specialist event-data, human-rights documentation, and comparative-law networks | Experienced evidence coders and adjudicators | Screen advocacy, government, supplier, and dataset conflicts case by case |

General-purpose crowd platforms may be used later for usability testing of the interface, not for gold political-event coding unless a participant independently satisfies the full specialist qualification gate.

Before selection, the administrator prepares a coverage matrix for research background, region expertise, professional reading languages, institution, and disclosed conflicts. The matrix supports assignments; it cannot become a demographic quality score or a reason to weaken qualifications.

## Draft public call

**Paid independent coders for a comparative political-event study**

Civica Atlas is preparing a blinded evaluation of its experimental Pulse event ledger. We are seeking researchers who can read political and institutional evidence, apply a versioned codebook, and explain uncertain judgments in writing.

The work covers sampled country-days and candidate events from a frozen 90-day period. Each packet is coded independently by two researchers. Coders do not see Civica's production label, model votes, owner decisions, or another coder's response. Disagreements go to a separately assigned adjudicator.

Applicants should have graduate training or equivalent professional experience in comparative politics, political science, international relations, public law, human rights, conflict studies, political-event data, or a related field. Please state the languages in which you can assess news and institutional evidence professionally. All required training, qualification tasks, assigned packets, and adjudication work are paid hourly.

The application asks for a short CV, relevant research or coding experience, language scope, availability, rate expectations, and conflict disclosures. Participation does not imply endorsement of Civica or its methods. Study results will report disagreements and limitations regardless of outcome.

This draft remains internal until the owner approves the budget and terms and the project reaches its external-contact gate.

## Owner approval and lead time

The owner must approve the candidate-pool mix, conflict policy, qualification gate, compensation posture, and hard budget ceiling before any contact. Allow six to eight weeks from contact authorization to a trained panel, then roughly nine to twelve weeks for base-plan coding and adjudication. External contact remains blocked until the master plan's G4 gate even if this package is approved earlier.

Contact authorization also requires PUL-041 to report that all three packet releases are complete and importable. Approval of this plan alone does not satisfy that data-readiness gate.
