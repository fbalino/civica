# Civica Index disposition resolution

**Release:** `civica-index-disposition-2026-07-v1`

## Selected product

The Governance Evidence Dashboard is the selected public comparison product. It keeps each publisher's observation on its native scale and shows ownership, definition, direction, vintage, uncertainty or its absence, missingness, provenance, and rights. It does not calculate a Civica country-quality composite, grade, or rank.

## Composite standing

K1 remains versioned research. Its code, frozen inputs, results, failures, and historical API evidence must be preserved. It is not an original Civica measurement or a recommended country ranking. The current league-table presentation failed the misuse audit.

The narrower derivative-use question remains open. A transparent summary might help qualified readers complete a defined task faster or more accurately. No human response data currently supports that claim. The project will not delete the research question or present it as settled.

## Evidence

The tournament package reproduces every candidate, baseline, and confirmatory result. The frozen decision has no winner. K1 fails originality, K2 fails stability, and the remaining candidate gates require qualified readers, coders, or specialists. Small final-holdout subgroup cells cannot establish subgroup accuracy.

## Arguments for retaining K1 research

A familiar scalar may lower reader burden. A derivative summary could still have bounded practical value even if it adds no information to its inputs. Preserving the code and failed results also makes later reconsideration auditable. These arguments justify retention and testing; they do not justify public recommendation.

## Reconsideration

K1 can return for a new versioned disposition only if it meets the frozen reader accuracy or completion-time threshold without losses in comprehension, source tracing, uncertainty awareness, or rejection of unsupported claims. Its presentation must also remove default league-table incentives and pass the misuse gate. Reliability, coverage, rights, and exact reproduction must remain passing. The current evidence cannot support a renewed original-measurement claim.

K2 through K5 require their declared external labels, coding reliability, citation checks, and specialist review before any can replace the dashboard as a public product.
