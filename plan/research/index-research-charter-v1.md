# Civica original-measurement research charter v1

**Adopted:** 2026-07-11

**Status:** Governing charter for the G3 Index candidate tournament

**Scope:** Any original Civica measure proposed as an Index successor or companion

## Purpose

Civica may develop original measurement only when it adds inspectable information that a reader cannot obtain from one established governance dataset or from a cosmetic recombination of established scores. The work serves comparative-politics researchers, data journalists, civil-society analysts, educators, and public-sector researchers who need citable cross-country evidence. It is not designed to tell voters which country is good, predict policy outcomes, allocate aid, price risk, or automate consequential decisions.

Eligible products may help a reader find and cite institutional facts, inspect where established sources agree or disagree, compare documented changes across time, identify records requiring closer investigation, or select evidence for a transparent analysis. No Civica output may be presented as sufficient grounds for sanctions, funding, immigration, policing, credit, insurance, employment, or electoral persuasion.

## Unit and cadence

Each candidate must declare exactly one primary unit before analysis: a jurisdiction-year/period observation, a documented event, an institutional state, a source-pair comparison, or a jurisdiction-construct-year concordance record. Cadence follows the underlying evidence: source-release, event-driven, annual, or frozen vintage. Mixed units require separate outputs rather than silent aggregation.

## Eligible constructs

Original Civica work must be grounded in provenance-native fact measurement or meta-measurement of documented agreement, disagreement, coverage, revision, or uncertainty among named projects. Established latent governance judgments may be displayed on their native scales with attribution. Reweighting or averaging them does not become an original Civica construct. Institutional structure is eligible only as a descriptive classification or documented state; it cannot become an overall country-quality verdict.

## Claim boundary

The research is descriptive unless a later, separately preregistered causal design justifies a causal claim. It does not estimate a country's overall worth, democratic legitimacy, governability, future stability, policy effectiveness, or moral standing. It does not establish that source agreement is truth, source disagreement is country failure, long tenure is autocracy, alternation is democracy, or constitutional text is practice.

No candidate may use letter grades, judgmental country labels, traffic-light verdicts, or one overall country rank. Every published result must retain its unit, inputs, vintage, missingness, transformation, uncertainty posture, methodology version, and correction path.

## Novelty requirement

A candidate contributes value only if its claimed result is not exactly recoverable from one upstream field or a disclosed arithmetic recombination of established judgment inputs, and it improves at least one preregistered task for a named target user over the strongest simple baseline without materially worsening provenance, comprehension, or misuse resistance. Novelty in branding, weights, normalization, interface, or update frequency is insufficient. A native-source dashboard may ship for reference value, but it is the no-original-measure baseline.

The frozen `ci-research-panel-2000-2024-v1` is the common longitudinal input where applicable. IDX-010 will preregister candidates, splits, baselines, metrics, exclusions, subgroup checks, multiplicity handling, and thresholds before winner-selecting analysis. Every candidate competes against the best single established indicator where coherent, a transparent equal-weight or first-factor baseline where coherent, and the source-native dashboard/no-score option. No candidate winning is an acceptable outcome.

Agent analysis can prepare, reproduce, stress-test, and falsify candidates. It cannot substitute for the independent expert and reader evidence required by later gates. Until G3 and G5 pass, every estimate remains research beta.

## Misuse controls

Every candidate specification must include foreseeable users who could be harmed, plausible misreadings, subgroup and coverage risks, consequential-decision risks, and a presentation test. Country pages must state what the result does not mean. Source attribution and limitations travel with API, download, embed, and citation forms. If readers systematically infer country quality after one presentation revision, the output is removed from country pages even if statistical tests pass.

## Falsification and retirement

A candidate is rejected before publication if it fails the information test, cannot beat its relevant simple baseline on its claimed use, lacks adequate source rights, cannot meet its reliability or validity threshold, or fails comprehension and misuse tests. Thresholds are fixed in IDX-010 and cannot be relaxed after results are seen.

A published original measure is versioned and re-evaluated annually and whenever its construct, rulebook, source set, model, or transformation materially changes. One failed annual gate triggers documented remediation and a visible limitation. Failure of the same required gate in two consecutive evaluations retires the output to a deprecated archive. A serious rights breach, unverifiable published result, or demonstrated consequential harm can trigger immediate suspension. Retirement preserves frozen evidence and citations; it does not rewrite history or silently replace the measure.

## Decision rule

The tournament may conclude with: ship as a descriptive research product; continue as a bounded research beta; publish only the source-native dashboard; or retire the candidate. The current four-input composite has no incumbency advantage. Final disposition requires the preregistered evidence packet and later external review, not owner preference or an agent vote.
