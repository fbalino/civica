## Why this option

The asks are substantial, specialist assignments rather than ordinary journal peer review. Each reviewer receives a version-pinned packet, answers a narrow methodological question, and produces a structured memo whose criticism and unresolved findings Civica preserves. Asking for 8–16 hours without compensation would narrow the pool toward people whose institutions can subsidize the work.

The proposed bands are legible and predictable. Comparable work receives the same amount, while the larger Pulse packet receives more. The implied rate is about $83–$125 per hour for an 8–12-hour review and about $94–$125 per hour for a 12–16-hour review. Those ranges sit above the modest $500 external-proposal honorarium published by Penn State and near the $750–$1,000 amounts commonly published for larger academic program reviews. They also recognize that Civica is asking for specialist methodological judgment, not symbolic service.

## Independence safeguards

Every offer and agreement must state that:

1. payment is for the agreed time and deliverable, never agreement with Civica or a favorable conclusion;
2. the full honorarium remains payable when the report is critical, recommends retirement, or leaves questions unresolved;
3. Civica preserves the submitted report and records its response separately;
4. public naming, quotation, or publication of the report requires separate explicit consent;
5. participation does not imply endorsement, authorship, advisory-board membership, or validation of unrelated claims;
6. the reviewer may disclose, recuse, or withdraw without penalty; completed work is paid pro rata if withdrawal follows a newly discovered conflict;
7. reviewers in the same scope band receive the same base offer, with no negotiation tied to prestige, viewpoint, country, or expected result; and
8. a source-project affiliation never permits the reviewer to be the sole judge of Civica's treatment of that source.

## Payment and logistics policy

Before the first offer, the owner must identify the legal paying person or entity and obtain accounting advice for that payer's jurisdiction. The operations record must then specify:

- whether the payment is an honorarium or contracted professional service under the applicable rules;
- the invoice or payee form required, payment currency and rail, who absorbs transfer fees, and the promised payment date;
- any tax form or withholding requirement. For example, an applicable US payer may need Form W-9 from a US person or Form W-8BEN from a foreign individual; the IRS says W-8BEN is supplied to the payer/withholding agent when required. This brief does not assume Civica has a US payer;
- whether a reviewer's employer requires prior approval, payment through the institution, refusal of personal payment, or donation of the amount;
- how institutional overhead is handled. It cannot silently reduce the reviewer's agreed base honorarium; any approved overhead must fit within the reserve or return for owner approval;
- a simple written scope, due date, cancellation term, confidentiality status, permitted publication, data access, deletion/retention duty, and conflict declaration; and
- accessible formats or other reasonable participation costs, approved separately and never deducted from the honorarium.

Payment should be initiated within 15 calendar days after the deliverable is accepted as complete against the agreed format. “Accepted” concerns delivery and completeness, not whether Civica likes the findings. If Civica cancels after work begins, it pays completed time pro rata, subject to a minimum of 25% of the agreed honorarium. A reviewer who submits the complete agreed deliverable receives the full fixed amount even if Civica does not use or publish it.

## Sources and limits

Sources checked 2026-07-11:

- [Penn State external expert review program](https://www.ems.psu.edu/research-faculty/resources/research-and-travel-grants/external-expert-review-program) — publishes a $500 honorarium and a three-to-four-week review window for critical proposal feedback.
- [Appalachian State summary of external grant-review programs](https://grs.appstate.edu/find-funding/become-peer-reviewer) — reports historical AmeriCorps honoraria up to $1,000 for an estimated 60–70 hours and BJA compensation of $125 per application; useful as evidence that public programs use very different units and rates, not as a direct price quote for Civica.
- [Ohio University academic program-review funding](https://www.ohio.edu/provost/academic-programs/program-reviews/funding) — publishes $1,000 for one external reviewer.
- [Cal Poly academic program review](https://academicprograms.calpoly.edu/content/phase-2-self-review) — states external reviewers are typically paid $1,000, excluding approved travel.
- [IRS Form W-8BEN guidance](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-8-ben) — confirms the form is given to the payer or withholding agent by a qualifying foreign beneficial owner when requested.

Published university honoraria are imperfect comparators: their scope, institutional subsidy, travel, prestige, and tax treatment differ. The proposed amounts are therefore a transparent Civica policy choice derived from the GOV-011 workload, not a claim about a universal market rate.
