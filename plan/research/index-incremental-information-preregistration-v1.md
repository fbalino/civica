**Protocol:** `civica-index-incremental-information/v1`  
**Status:** locked before prediction results

K1 is tested on complete final-holdout jurisdiction-years. Every fitted comparator uses development rows only. The common-sample models are native V-Dem LDI, the frozen equal-weight baseline, the frozen first-factor baseline, and ordinary least squares over the four public normalized K1 inputs. Each scalar baseline receives only a development-fitted intercept and slope. No final-holdout row can affect a fitted parameter.

If the four public inputs reproduce K1 with final-holdout R² of at least 0.90, K1 fails the tournament's original-information gate. High reproduction accuracy is adverse evidence for originality.

This test does not establish decision utility. That requires the separately frozen human-task improvement of at least 10 percentage points or 20% lower median time with no comprehension loss.
