# Civica Index adversarial normative and misuse audit

**Release:** `index-misuse-audit-v1`

## Resolution

The current public presentation fails the misuse-resistance gate. This finding does not automatically retire every possible derivative composite. It means the present default ranking table, exact ordering, and API-ready single number create risks that the methodology evidence cannot support.

Eight threat families were tested through comparative-measurement, cross-cultural, journalism, public-policy, and data-poor-state lenses. Each has a concrete mitigation and a suspension or retirement trigger in the machine-readable result.

## Highest risks

The public rankings table defaults to descending Civica Index and the metadata advertises country rankings. This is a direct league-table incentive. Plausible choices can move the 95th percentile of ranks by 10–33 places, and there is no calibrated composite confidence interval. Exact adjacent positions therefore carry false precision.

The total also compresses a particular liberal-governance tradition into one better-to-worse axis. That tradition can be named and studied, but it should not be presented as a culture-neutral total of governance quality. Geographically diverse review is required before any bounded construct claim is strengthened.

A compact score and API field are easy to reuse in aid, sanctions, procurement, migration, credit, or automated risk systems. No current validation supports those uses. Consequential use must be explicitly prohibited, and a documented consequential deployment triggers suspension.

Historical backcasts add another hazard. V-Dem edition changes alter 526 rounded K1 scores, and the older BR event-label editions are not stable enough to be treated as timeless. Every cited output must carry exact input editions, vintage, and a reconstruction hash.

## Data-poor states

The narrow scarcity simulation does not show an automatic poor-governance penalty. Coverage and uncertainty are still unequal: small states and the missing-media group have fewer publisher-bounded inputs, and one sovereign state is withheld. Every surviving result must display source count and missingness. Comparisons with materially unequal evidence should be suppressed rather than made visually equivalent.

## Required disposition

K1 may remain versioned internal research while the tournament continues. It must not be a recommended country ranking, a best/worst product, a consequential-use field, or an original or independently corroborated measurement claim. Secondary public use requires removal of default league-table treatment, published sensitivity, exact vintage binding, a consequential-use prohibition, qualified-reader nonclaim testing, and geographically diverse external review.
