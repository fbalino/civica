# K3 Power and Transfer Ledger validation protocol v1

## Reliability

Two independent coders apply the frozen rulebook to a stratified sample covering executive-system families, regions, disputed elections, acting officeholders, indirect selection, coalitions, monarchies, and collective executives. Krippendorff's alpha must reach the threshold frozen in the tournament contract for executive identity, transfer classification, and term-limit status. Disagreements are adjudicated after the blind score is recorded.

## Citation audit

A seeded stratified random sample of prototype rows is traced from every displayed field to its statement URL and retained source hash. The preregistered verifiability threshold applies to the whole row. A missing or mismatched citation fails the row; it cannot be excused by another correct field.

## Historical comparison

After predecessor terms and election links exist, the overlap sample is compared with Archigos and NELDA under their compatible units and vintages. Every disagreement enters a public adjudication log. Agreement below the frozen threshold blocks derived transfer publication.

## Prospective freshness

New real-world executive transfers are observed prospectively for the frozen number of quarters. The clock starts at the documented transfer date and stops when a fully cited ledger event is ready. Missing the frozen share-within-days target removes the “living dataset” claim and restricts publication to named vintages.

## Current disposition

The validation has not run. The prototype can test current-tenure extraction and citation plumbing only. Historical transfer and term-limit fields remain unavailable until the necessary source histories are built and the independent gates pass.
