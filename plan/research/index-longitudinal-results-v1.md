## Responsiveness

Fifty-five BR democracy transitions have usable K1 endpoints. K1 moves in the coded direction for 74.5% of them (95% jurisdiction-cluster bootstrap interval 63.3%–85.0%). Median signed movement from the year before through the year after a transition is five points (interval three to seven). Both frozen responsiveness thresholds pass.

The mean signed annual movement is −0.49 at lag −2, 0.46 at −1, 4.36 in the transition year, 1.14 one year later, and −0.02 two years later. The response peaks at lag zero, as preregistered.

## Quiet-period noise

Across 2,890 annual periods with unchanged BR state and no adjacent transition, median absolute K1 movement is one point and the 95th percentile is four. Event movement is five times the quiet-period median. All frozen quiet-noise gates pass.

Quiet BR coding does not prove that no institutional change occurred. This comparison only establishes that K1 is usually more active near this particular external transition definition than during its matched nontransition periods.

Lag-one autocorrelation is 0.9965 for score levels and 0.1107 for annual changes. Country ordering is extremely persistent, while adjacent annual movements have little serial dependence. The level statistic therefore cannot stand in for change reliability.

## Revision sensitivity

Replacing V-Dem v15 LDI with exact v14 values while holding other K1 inputs fixed yields 3,088 comparable scores. The median absolute score shift is zero, the 95th percentile is one point, and the maximum is two. The V-Dem revision gate passes. There are 526 nonzero shifts, so a displayed annual point can still change solely because a source release changed.

BR event labels are less stable across the longer edition gap. QoG Jan25 and Jan26 event sets have 99.2% Jaccard agreement over their common years. Jan24 and Jan26 have 84.9%, below the frozen 90% threshold. The 39 additional or revised common-window events mean the known-change benchmark itself is version-sensitive. Every longitudinal result must name the BR edition and should not combine editions as one fixed gold standard.

## Missingness and limits

The frozen BR grid contains 105 missing labels. Of 264 Jan26 transitions across its full historical coverage, 209 lack the K1 t−1/t+1 endpoints required by this 2000–2022 analysis. No endpoint is imputed.

The results support responsiveness to BR-coded transitions and low typical quiet-period noise. They do not establish causality, cover every kind of institutional change, or prove that K1 changes form one construct. The dimensionality result still shows that annual changes are multidimensional.
