# Civica free typeface alternatives

- **Verified:** 2026-07-26
- **Scope:** private development Type Lab only
- **Production default:** unchanged — Source Serif 4 + Inter

All free candidates below use the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Similarity is a
specimen-based design judgment, not metric compatibility.

| Paid Type Lab candidate | Free comparison now in the lab | Why it is useful |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Lyon Display](https://commercialtype.com/catalog/lyon_display) | [Cormorant Garamond](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond) | Closest free French-Renaissance display character; more ornamental and higher-contrast than Lyon. |
| [Signifier](https://klim.co.nz/fonts/signifier/) | [Instrument Serif](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif) | Sharp contemporary display serif; much smaller family than Signifier. |
| [Tiempos Fine / Headline](https://klim.co.nz/collections/tiempos/) | [Newsreader](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/newsreader) | Serious editorial oldstyle with an optical-size axis; less fine-cut than Tiempos. |
| [Martina Plantijn](https://klim.co.nz/fonts/martina-plantijn/) | [EB Garamond](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ebgaramond) | Warm historical book character; narrower and more archival than Martina. |
| [Suisse Works](https://www.swisstypefaces.com/fonts/suisse/) | [Source Serif 4](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/sourceserif4) | The existing free system remains the strongest low-risk operational substitute. |
| [ABC Diatype](https://abcdinamo.com/typefaces/diatype) | [Instrument Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentsans) | Precise contemporary grotesque with more warmth than Inter; the closest free UI comparison. |
| [Suisse Int’l](https://www.swisstypefaces.com/fonts/suisse/) | [Work Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/worksans) | Flexible grotesque with a less neutral, more human texture. |
| [National](https://klim.co.nz/fonts/national/) | [Public Sans](https://github.com/uswds/public-sans) | Clear civic/public-sector voice; less idiosyncratic than National. |
| [Metric](https://klim.co.nz/fonts/metric/) | [Source Sans 3](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/sourcesans3) | Broad, readable interface workhorse; functionally closer than it is visually identical. |
| [Arbeit Pro](https://supply.family/shop/arbeit-pro/) | [Archivo](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/archivo) | Robust, compact grotesque with similar institutional force. |
| [Centra No.2](https://www.sharptype.co/typefaces/centra-no-2) | [Manrope](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope) | Geometric-humanist warmth and high x-height; softer and more circular than Centra. |

## Pair conclusions

1. **Closest free analogue to Lyon + Diatype:** Cormorant Garamond +
   Instrument Sans.
2. **Lowest-risk free option:** Source Serif 4 + Instrument Sans.
3. **Most civic/institutional:** Newsreader + Public Sans.

outside the repository. No font in this note is approved as a production
