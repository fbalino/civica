# Civica external-review roles and selection criteria

**Contract:** `civica-reviewer-selection/v1`

**Adopted:** 2026-07-11

**Status:** names-free pre-contact policy

No one has been contacted. Quiet research may use public professional evidence, but no invitation or availability inquiry may occur until GOV-016 passes and the owner approves the contact-ready list.

## Purpose

External reviewers will answer bounded questions about versioned artifacts. They are not endorsing Civica, joining the advisory board, becoming authors, or validating every claim. Selection rests on exact task competence, independence, a manageable conflict record, and the ability to produce an evidence-based report.

The machine-readable contract at `data/research/reviewer-selection-criteria-v1.json` is authoritative for the detailed minimums, conflicts, diversity rules, deliverables, exclusions, and source links. This document explains how those rules will be applied.

## Shared rules

Candidates need a current public professional identity and recent lane-specific work, or a current standards/implementation role that establishes equivalent expertise. Public institutional pages, publications, project documentation, standards roles, and professional contact pages are allowed evidence. Personal phone numbers, private addresses, non-public email addresses, inferred protected traits, and availability guesses are prohibited.

Every candidate must disclose financial, professional, personal, institutional, source-project, and competitive relationships before receiving confidential material. An adjacent source affiliation can be disclosed and managed. A relationship that overlaps the assigned judgment requires recusal and a non-affiliated reviewer for that judgment. Current Civica authorship or decision authority, outcome-contingent compensation, an unmanageable financial conflict, or refusal to disclose excludes the person.

COPE's reviewer guidance informs scope matching, objectivity, confidentiality, conflicts, and timeliness. It does not turn Civica's process into journal peer review. Original reports, conflicts, recusals, author responses, rejected changes, and unresolved disagreements will be retained under GOV-018.

## Review lanes

### `governance_measurement`

This core scholarly lane reviews the GOV-014 Index tournament packet. Candidates need methods work on cross-national governance measurement, construct validity, aggregation, latent variables, uncertainty, or comparative-index evaluation. The panel must include source-project implementation knowledge and an outside critical perspective; nobody may be the sole judge of their own measure or Civica's treatment of it. The deliverable is a structured memo on the frozen tournament and its no-winner/source-native disposition.

### `political_event_data`

This core scholarly lane reviews the GOV-015 Pulse packet. Candidates need hands-on event-data construction, coding, validation, or methodological critique, with relevant work on retrieval, selection bias, deduplication, coder reliability, machine/human comparison, attribution, or calibration. Dataset builders must be balanced with error-analysis critics and regional/language perspectives. The deliverable covers construct, ontology, sampling, observability, coding, metrics, subgroup limits, and the prospective protocol.

### `research_data_curation`

This core scholarly lane reviews the GOV-013 Atlas packet and the reproducibility sections of the experimental-product packets. Candidates need research-data curation, repository certification, FAIR implementation, preservation, data citation, or reproducibility-review practice. They must attempt the release as a designated user, including clean-room reconstruction and link/checksum checks. General open-source advocacy without research-data practice is insufficient.

### `accessibility`

This professional lane reviews a commit-pinned live-site scope using WCAG-EM. Knowledgeable human evaluation is required; an automated scan is not a conformance review. The panel combines evaluation skill with direct disability or assistive-technology user perspective without demanding anyone's private medical information. It tests named country, table, map, chart, search, citation, export, and correction tasks and separates conformance failures, usability findings, and tool warnings.

### `legal_source_rights`

This professional lane reviews the rights and source-input manifests, licensing and terms, exports, embeds, code posture, and release bundle. Candidates need database-rights, copyright, open-data, research-reuse, or non-commercial-license expertise. Scholarship can identify and analyze issues; release legal advice requires counsel professionally qualified for the applicable jurisdiction. Source-provider representation is disclosed and can require source-specific recusal. The deliverable is a source-by-source issue table plus a signed statement of scope, uncertainty, and unresolved permissions.

## Panel composition and later ranking

GOV-009 must retain at least eight publicly evidenced candidates for each of the three core scholarly lanes. GOV-010 must preserve at least three alternates per lane. Each core lane should retain qualified perspectives outside the US and EU/UK when the public pool permits, more than one method or institutional network, and hands-on mid-career or practitioner candidates alongside senior scholars. If a diversity search fails, the record will show the search and gap; expertise thresholds will not be lowered or identities inferred.

Names, affiliations, contact routes, and current activity belong in GOV-009. Scores and ranking belong in GOV-010. Personalized asks and honorarium terms belong in GOV-011/GOV-012. This separation prevents a preferred name from rewriting the criteria after the fact.
