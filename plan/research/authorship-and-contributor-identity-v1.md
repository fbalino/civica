## Adding contributors

A new person is added only with consent and a record of:

- supplied name and public identity;
- affiliation or independent status;
- ORCID if the person provides one;
- one or more exact role ids;
- contribution period and artifact evidence; and
- a public contact route or consented project contact route.

Source publishers remain attributed as sources. Independent reviewers remain credited as reviewers when they consent. Neither becomes a Civica author without a qualifying authorship contribution and explicit consent. Prior contribution records remain preserved when a role changes.

The machine-readable record is `data/research/authorship-and-contributions-v1.json`. Canonical citation files name Fernando personally and list Civica Atlas as publisher.
