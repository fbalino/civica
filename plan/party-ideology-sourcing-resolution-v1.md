# Party-Ideology Sourcing Resolution v1

**Status:** Research + feasibility probe. Owner has already chosen V-Party (V-Dem)
as the source. This document assesses the dataset, measures the real match rate
against Civica's existing party data, and specifies the provenance, gap-handling,
and build plan. No code changed, nothing ingested, nothing committed.

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**Author:** research agent (feasibility probe)
**Feature it powers:** cross-country political-party browser + ideology compass
(mockup: `mockups/04-18-2026-political-party-browser.html`)
**Discipline:** produced as a citable methodology artifact per the 2026-05-02
research-lab decision. Every external claim carries a URL.

---

## 1. Current state

Civica already stores parties, but with **no ideology attribute and no external
identity spine**. Measured live from the database on 2026-07-06:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Rows in `legislature_parties` | **1,548** |
| Distinct legislative bodies (`body_id`) | 194 |
| Distinct countries (via `body → government_bodies.jurisdiction_id → jurisdictions`) | **141** |
| Total seats summed across all rows | 43,563 |
| Rows with a `wikidata_qid` | **0** (column is empty for every row) |

Continent split of the 1,548 rows: Europe 478 · Asia 422 · Africa 353 ·
North America 144 · South America 86 · Oceania 65.

Columns present: `id, body_id, party_name, party_color, seat_count,
is_ruling_coalition, wikidata_qid`. Because `wikidata_qid` is empty, **there is no
key to join on** — ideology must be matched by **party name + country** against an
external source. The mockup's compass, ideology filter, and "Source: V-Party
Dataset" caption all depend on that match succeeding at a defensible rate.

A meaningful share of the 1,548 rows are **not single codeable parties**: they are
aggregate/procedural buckets like `Others`, `Independents`, `Crossbench`,
`Divers`, `CDU/CSU-led states`, `Ensemble (presidential majority)`,
`Elected members`, `Party-list representatives`. The probe flags ~90 such rows;
they can never carry an ideology position and are excluded from the "codeable"
denominator below.

## 2. V-Party (V-Dem) assessment

### 2.1 What the dataset is

**Varieties of Party Identity and Organization (V-Party)** — a V-Dem Institute
dataset of expert-coded policy positions and organizational attributes for
political parties worldwide.

- **Current version: V-Party v2, released February 2022.**
  ([V-Dem V-Party page](https://www.v-dem.net/data/v-party-dataset/),
  [Country-Party-Date v2](https://www.v-dem.net/data/v-party-dataset/country-party-date-v2/))
- **Coverage:** 1970–2019; **1,955 parties across 1,560 elections in 169
  countries**, ~6,330 party-election-year units, ≥4 expert coders per observation.
  ([QoG DataFinder — V-Party](https://datafinder.qog.gu.se/dataset/vparty))
- **The vintage ceiling is 2019.** This is the single most important property for
  us: any party that first won seats, was renamed, or was formed after 2019 is
  **absent by construction** (see §3 failure modes).

### 2.2 Access & format

- Download package (ZIP) includes **Dataset, Codebook, Party Coding Units,
  What's New, Cautionary Notes, Suggested Citations**. Formats offered:
  **CSV, Stata (.dta), R (.rds), SPSS (.sav)** — the QoG mirror lists
  "SPSS, STATA, CSV, R" for the Country-Party-Date file.
  ([V-Dem V-Party page](https://www.v-dem.net/data/v-party-dataset/),
  [QoG DataFinder](https://datafinder.qog.gu.se/dataset/vparty))
- Programmatic access: the **`vdemdata` R package**
  ([github.com/vdeminstitute/vdemdata](https://github.com/vdeminstitute/vdemdata))
  bundles the data; QoG DataFinder offers a variable-picker download.
- **Party identity is harmonized to Party Facts** (Döring & Regel 2019). Every
  V-Party party carries a Party Facts core-party ID, which is what made the
  match-rate probe in §3 possible without pulling the full V-Dem ZIP: Party
  Facts publishes the V-Party party list (country + names + years) as an open
  CSV keyed `dataset_key = 'vparty'`
  ([partyfacts.herokuapp.com download](https://partyfacts.herokuapp.com/download/external-parties-csv/)).

### 2.3 License (verified, verbatim)

- V-Dem FAQ: **"All V-Dem data and graphs generated by the online tools are open
  source and free for anyone to use."** The only obligation is the mandatory
  dataset citation. **No non-commercial restriction is stated.**
  ([V-Dem FAQ](https://www.v-dem.net/about/faq/))
- The V-Dem dataset download pages describe the data as released under the
  **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC-BY-SA)** family and
  "downloadable free of charge."
  ([The V-Dem Dataset](https://www.v-dem.net/data/the-v-dem-dataset/))
- **Posture for Civica: commercial use IS allowed** (CC-BY-SA / "free for anyone
  to use"), with attribution + share-alike. This is a *materially better* license
  than Civica's other party-related sources: IPU Parline, Constitute, and IDEA
  turnout are all CC-BY-NC-SA (non-commercial) and are flagged as
  relicense-or-remove-if-Civica-ever-charges (see memory-decisions 2026-07-05).
  **V-Party carries no such flag** — it does not add to the non-commercial debt.
- **Required attribution** (the V-Party-specific suggested citation ships inside
  the download ZIP; the V-Dem house citation is: Coppedge et al. 2026, "V-Dem
  Dataset v16," Varieties of Democracy Project — the V-Party ZIP provides the
  parallel Lührmann/Düpont/Higashijima et al. V-Party citation to use).
  ([V-Dem FAQ](https://www.v-dem.net/about/faq/))

### 2.4 Key variables for a compass

Extracted from the **V-Party v2 codebook**
([vparty_codebook_v2.pdf](https://www.v-dem.net/documents/6/vparty_codebook_v2.pdf)).
Every position variable ships in multiple forms: `*_ord` (ordinal category),
`*_osp` / `*_mean` (interval point estimate from the Bayesian IRT model), and
`*_codelow`/`*_codehigh` (credible interval). Use the interval point estimate for
plotting and the ordinal for labeling.

**Economic axis**
| Code | Measures | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| `v2pariglef` | **Economic left–right** — active-state/redistribution (left) vs. reduced-state/privatization (right) | ordinal 0 Far-left … 3 Center … 6 Far-right; interval IRT version for plotting |
| `v2pawelf` | Welfare: means-tested vs. universalistic | ordinal 0–5 |
| `v2paclient` | Clientelism (targeted/excludable goods for votes) | interval |

**Liberal ↔ illiberal / social axis (candidate second axis)**
| Code | Measures | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| `v2xpa_antiplural` | **Anti-Pluralism Index** — lacking commitment to democratic norms; a composite of opponent-demonization, pluralism, minority rights, and rejection of violence | interval 0 (pluralist) – 1 (anti-pluralist) |
| `v2xpa_popul` | Populism Index — populist rhetoric (anti-elitism × people-centrism), harmonic mean | interval 0–1 |
| `v2paanteli` | Anti-elitism | interval |
| `v2papeople` | People-centrism | interval |
| `v2paopresp` | Demonization of political opponents | interval |
| `v2paplur` | Commitment to political pluralism | interval |
| `v2paminor` | Minority rights | interval |
| `v2paviol` | Rejection of political violence | interval |
| `v2paimmig` | Immigration (restrictive ↔ open) | interval |
| `v2paculsup` | Cultural superiority / nativism | interval |
| `v2parelig` | Religious principles in policy | interval |

**Identity / matching variables** (all sourced from Party Facts, Döring & Regel 2019)
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `v2paid` | Numeric party identifier = Party Facts core-party ID |
| `v2paenname` | Harmonized **English** party name |
| `v2paorname` | Harmonized **original-language** party name |
| `v2pashname` | Harmonized **short name / abbreviation** (e.g. `SPD`, `CDU`, `PT`) |
| `pf_party_id`, `pf_url` | Party Facts ID + URL |
| `country_name`, `country_text_id` (ISO3) | Coded country |
| `year`, `historical_date` | Election-specific dates (a party appears once per election it contested) |

The presence of **English name + original name + short name + ISO3 country +
year** is exactly the surface a name+country matcher needs, and is why the probe
in §3 works.

### 2.5 Recommended compass axes

**Recommendation: `v2pariglef` (economic left–right) on the X-axis ×
`v2xpa_antiplural` (Anti-Pluralism / liberal–illiberal) on the Y-axis.**

Rationale:
- **X = economic left–right** is the single most widely understood, most defensible
  party-placement axis (Downsian left–right; the standard first dimension in CHES,
  Manifesto, and V-Party's own party-system indices). It maps 1:1 onto the
  mockup's "Left ↔ Right" horizontal axis and the ideology filter buckets
  (Far-Left … Far-Right).
- **Y = Anti-Pluralism** is the axis where V-Party is *strongest and most
  distinctive* — it is what V-Party was built to measure — and it corresponds to
  the mockup's vertical "Authoritarian ↔ Libertarian" axis honestly (a
  pluralist/illiberal axis, not a GAL-TAN culture axis V-Party doesn't cleanly
  provide). It is a published composite index with credible intervals, so it is
  defensible to plot and to cite.
- We should **relabel the mockup's vertical axis** from "Authoritarian ↔
  Libertarian" to **"Pluralist ↔ Anti-pluralist"** (or "Liberal ↔ Illiberal") so
  the axis label matches what the data actually measures. Do not present
  `v2xpa_popul` as the vertical axis — populism is a rhetoric measure, not a
  left-right-orthogonal ideology axis, and would mislead.
- A GAL-TAN-style culture axis (immigration/nativism/religion) is available from
  `v2paimmig` / `v2paculsup` / `v2parelig` as an *optional third lens later*, but
  it is not a single published index in v2, so it should not be the v1 compass axis.

Both chosen variables are **published, IRT-estimated, and interval-scaled with
credible intervals** — good enough to plot dots and, if we want, error ellipses.

## 3. Feasibility — measured match rate

**This is the number that decides whether the compass is worth building.**

### 3.1 Method

1. Pulled the **live Civica party list** (1,548 rows) with country ISO3 via the
   `legislature_parties → government_bodies → jurisdictions` join.
2. Reconstructed the **V-Party party universe** from Party Facts' open external-
   parties CSV filtered to `dataset_key = 'vparty'`: **3,489 party records across
   178 country codes**, each with English name, original name, short name/abbrev,
   ISO3 country, and first/last coded year. (V-Party party identity *is* Party
   Facts, per §2.4, so this is the authentic V-Party party list, not a proxy.)
3. Matched each Civica party **within the same country** using: exact normalized
   English/original/short name; parenthetical-abbreviation extraction
   (`"Christian Democratic Union (CDU)"` → `CDU`) against `v2pashname`;
   short-name-as-name; and a stop-word-tolerant token-overlap fallback
   (Jaccard ≥ 0.6 or full containment). Accents stripped, punctuation removed.

### 3.2 Results (measured, not estimated)

| Denominator | Matched | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **All 1,548 rows** | 638 | **41.2%** |
| **Codeable rows** (excl. Others/Independents/aggregates, n=1,458) | 638 | **43.8%** |
| **Seat-weighted** (matched seats ÷ 43,563 total) | 27,044 | **62.1%** |
| **Major parties** (top-5 by seats per body, codeable, n=748) | 422 | **56.4%** |

By region (codeable rows): Africa 50% · North America 50% · Oceania 42% ·
Europe 41% · Asia 41% · South America 34%.

Country coverage: of Civica's **141 countries, 127 have V-Party data**. The 14
with **no** V-Party coverage are all expected exclusions — micro-states and
non-competitive systems: Antigua & Barbuda, The Bahamas, Belize, Kiribati,
Liechtenstein, Nauru, St. Lucia, St. Vincent, Samoa, San Marino, Saudi Arabia,
South Sudan, Tonga, Tuvalu.

### 3.3 How to read these numbers

**The compass is worth building.** The headline metric for a *compass* is not the
raw row rate — it is the **seat-weighted and major-party rate**, because the
compass plots the parties that hold power and that readers look for. At **~62% of
seats and ~56% of the top-5 parties per chamber matched by an automated matcher**,
every marquee party in the mockup lands: Germany's CDU/SPD/AfD/Greens/Linke/CSU,
Brazil's PT/PSDB/PMDB/PL, India's BJP/INC, South Africa's ANC/DA, UK Labour, etc.
all match cleanly (verified by spot-check).

The 41–44% *row* rate is deflated by three honest, well-understood causes — **not
by bad matching**:

**Failure mode A — the 2019 vintage ceiling (dominant, ~half the major-party
misses).** Parties formed or renamed after 2019 cannot match:
- Renames: **National Rally** (RN, renamed from Front National in 2018 — V-Party
  has it as "FN / National Front" through 2017), **People Power Party** (Korea,
  2020), **ANO 2011** (V-Party has "ANO").
- New parties: **TISZA** (Hungary, 2024), **Pastef** (Senegal), **Citizens
  Coalition for Change** (Zimbabwe, 2022), **Rastriya Swatantra Party** (Nepal),
  **Prosperity Party** (Ethiopia, 2019 merger).
- Electoral coalitions Civica stores as one row while V-Party codes the component
  parties: **New Popular Front**, **Ensemble** (France), multi-party Italian
  lists.

**Failure mode B — one-party / non-competitive legislatures (genuinely
uncodeable).** China's Chinese Communist Party (2,090 seats), North Korea's
Workers' Party (607), Vietnam's Communist Party (482). V-Party codes *multiparty
competition*; these have no left-right compass position by design and should never
be plotted on the compass regardless of source.

**Failure mode C — matcher-level residue (recoverable, modest).** Stemming/plural
gaps ("Conservatives" in V-Party vs. "Conservative Party" in Civica),
transliteration variants, and original-language-only names. A production matcher
adding a light stemmer, `v2paorname` original-language matching, and a **one-time
manual review of the top-N-by-seats unmatched parties** would recover several more
points — realistically landing the **seat-weighted rate in the ~65–70% band**.
This is a bounded, one-pass curation cost, not an open-ended one.

**Honesty note:** these are *real* numbers from a real probe. The compass will
show a strong-but-partial world: the parties in power in competitive democracies
are well covered; recent insurgent parties and one-party states are not. That is
the truthful story to tell in the UI (§5) and the methodology page.

## 4. Provenance & storage plan

### 4.1 New `sources` row

Add one row via `scripts/seed-sources.ts` (mirrors the existing `vdem` /
`vdem_pulse` rows; V-Party is a distinct dataset with its own version and citation
so it gets its own id, not a reuse of `vdem`):

```
id:                       "vparty"
name:                     "V-Dem — Varieties of Party Identity and Organization (V-Party) v2"
baseUrl:                  "https://www.v-dem.net/data/v-party-dataset/"
license:                  "CC-BY-SA"
isCommercialUseAllowed:   true            // ← unlike IPU/IDEA/Constitute
```

`SourceDot` treats this as a **frozen** vintage (amber) — V-Party v2 is a fixed
2022 release covering through 2019, not a live feed (consistent with the
"frozen academic/quarterly vintages = amber" rule in memory-sessions).

### 4.2 Where matched positions live

**Recommendation: a new `party_positions` table, not new columns on
`legislature_parties`.** Reasons: (a) `legislature_parties` is a
seat-snapshot table refreshed by legislature syncs — bolting ideology columns onto
it would entangle two different vintages and two different sync cadences;
(b) a separate table lets us store the match provenance (which V-Party record,
what confidence, what year) and swap in a v3 dataset later without touching seat
data; (c) it keeps the "codeable vs. matched vs. unmatched" state explicit.

Proposed shape (Drizzle, `src/lib/db/schema.ts`):

```
party_positions {
  id                uuid pk
  legislature_party_id  uuid → legislature_parties.id   // the Civica row matched
  source_id         text → sources.id                    // "vparty"
  vparty_id         integer        // v2paid / Party Facts core id
  vparty_name_en    text           // v2paenname, for auditability
  econ_left_right   real           // v2pariglef point estimate (X axis)
  econ_lr_ord       integer        // v2pariglef_ord 0–6 (label bucket)
  anti_pluralism    real           // v2xpa_antiplural 0–1 (Y axis)
  populism          real           // v2xpa_popul 0–1 (optional)
  coded_year        integer        // the V-Party election year used
  match_method      text           // 'exact' | 'abbrev' | 'token' | 'manual'
  match_confidence  text           // 'high' | 'review' — 'review' surfaced in an admin queue
  created_at        timestamp
}
```

Keying by `legislature_party_id` (not by name) means the ideology attaches to a
specific Civica party row and travels with it. Store the **most recent V-Party
election year** per party (prefer latest `coded_year`), and keep `vparty_id` +
`coded_year` so every plotted dot traces to an exact V-Party observation — this is
the "provenance is load-bearing" invariant.

### 4.3 Sync discipline

The ingestion script (`scripts/sync-vparty-positions.ts`, `npm run sync:vparty`)
**must** stamp freshness via `markSourcesSynced("vparty", { rowsWritten })` from
`src/lib/db/source-freshness.ts` — never write `last_sync_at` inline (enforced by
`npm run validate:sync-freshness`). Because V-Party v2 is frozen, the sync is
effectively a one-time load + re-runnable curation pass; `markSourcesSynced` only
stamps when rows are actually written, so a dry run or empty pass won't fake
freshness.

## 5. Gap strategy & honesty treatment

**Rule: never fabricate a position.** A party with no V-Party match gets *no dot on
the compass and no left-right bucket* — full stop. Concretely:

- **In the browser list:** unmatched parties still appear (they hold real seats),
  with an explicit **"Ideology not recorded"** chip in place of the ideology tag,
  and no compass dot. Use the canonical `<Chip variant="neutral">` — do not invent
  a styling.
- **On the compass:** only matched, codeable parties are plotted. The compass
  caption states the honest coverage, e.g. *"Plotting 638 of 1,548 parties with a
  V-Party position (≈62% of seats). Recent and single-party-state parties are
  listed but not plotted."* This mirrors the mockup's existing "Source: V-Party
  Dataset · 163 countries" caption but makes it truthful.
- **One-party states:** parties like the CCP are shown in the list flagged
  "Not applicable — non-competitive legislature," never "not recorded" (a
  different, correct reason).
- **Methodology page:** a short `/parties/methodology` (or a section under the
  existing methodology tree) states the source, the 2019 vintage ceiling, the
  match method, the measured coverage, and the two axes — inheriting V-Party's
  external standing, so **no BETA pill** is needed (per the 2026-05-02 rule:
  citing an external methodology, not asserting a novel Civica composite).

**Wikidata P1142 (political ideology) / P1387 (political alignment) as a fallback?**
**Recommendation: v1 = V-Party only. Document Wikidata as a *possible* secondary
lens, but do not ship it in v1.** Reasons: (a) V-Party is a single, citable,
peer-reviewed, interval-scaled source with credible intervals — mixing in
crowd-sourced Wikidata ideology strings on the *same* compass would blend two
incompatible provenance standards and undermine the academic posture; (b) our
`wikidata_qid` column is empty, so a Wikidata fallback would first require solving
the *same* name→QID matching problem, for a lower-quality payoff; (c) Wikidata
ideologies are free-text labels, not a left-right coordinate, so they can't be
plotted on the compass anyway. If we later want to *raise list coverage* (not
compass coverage), Wikidata P1142 could annotate the "ideology not recorded"
rows with a clearly separate, second `<SourceDot>` — but that is a v2 decision,
gated on its own mini-resolution.

## 6. Build outline (S / M / L)

**S — Ingestion + matcher + data (small-to-medium, ~1 focused pass).**
- `scripts/sync-vparty-positions.ts`: load V-Party v2 (CSV from the V-Dem ZIP, or
  Party Facts join for the identity spine), run the name+country matcher
  (§3.1 logic, plus a stemmer and `v2paorname` matching), write `party_positions`,
  stamp `markSourcesSynced`. Add `party_positions` to `schema.ts` + a Drizzle
  migration + the `vparty` `sources` row in `seed-sources.ts`.
- One-time **manual review** of top-N-by-seats "review"-confidence matches (a
  small admin/CSV pass) to lift the seat-weighted rate toward ~65–70%.

**M — Party browser page + ideology compass UI (the main build).**
- New reader surface `/parties` (list + filters + compass), built **entirely on
  design-system primitives** — `EditorialPage`/reader layout, `Chip` for ideology
  tags and the "not recorded" state, `SegmentedControl` for region/ideology
  toggles, `DataTable` for the list view, `SourceDot` on every ideology value.
  The compass itself is a new **design-system component** (scatter on the two
  V-Party axes) added to `globals.css` + `/design-system` + DESIGN.md *first* —
  no page-local styling, no hard-coded colors (per the owner mandate). Axis labels
  = "Left ↔ Right" (X) and "Pluralist ↔ Anti-pluralist" (Y).
- Query layer: `getPartiesWithPositions()` joining `legislature_parties` +
  `party_positions` + jurisdiction, with seat/coverage aggregates for the caption.

**L — Cross-country party families, per-country integration, embeds.**
- Wire the compass/ideology chip into the country page's Civica-Data legislature
  block (each party row gets its position + `SourceDot`).
- "Party family" groupings (the mockup's Social-Democratic-family featured card) —
  derive from `v2pariglef` buckets or a party-family field; methodology-gated.
- Optional third culture-axis lens (`v2paimmig`/`v2paculsup`/`v2parelig`) and any
  API/embed exposure.
