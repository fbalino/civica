**Status:** Complete

## What this proves

The report records a dated, contact-free landscape for `Civica Atlas`,
`Civica`, close variants, and adjacent governance-atlas names. It covers:

- active organizations and products;
- relevant geographies, sectors, and audiences;
- official marks and company records supplied by BRD-002;
- domains, public social identities, and search-result behavior;
- verbal, service, audience, channel, and geographic overlap; and
- both observed actual-confusion evidence and potential indicators.

The review found no documented incident of actual user confusion and no
third-party governance-data product using the exact two-word name in the
bounded search. It also found meaningful practical overlap with Civica UK
Limited’s public-sector software/data activity and registered `CIVICA` word
mark, plus research/education overlap with CIVICA, the European University of
Social Sciences.

## Neutrality and limits

- Competitors and namesakes are described factually and without disparagement.
- Observations, practical inferences, and legal questions are separated.
- No survey, support-log study, interview, market test, or contact with a
  namesake was performed.
- No inference is made from unavailable or privately registered domain data.
- The report is not trademark clearance or legal advice.
- BRD-003 remains the mandatory professional-review gate.
