# IDX-007 evidence — original-measurement research charter

## Adopted boundary

The governing charter is `plan/research/index-research-charter-v1.md`. It names five target-user groups, the decisions Civica evidence may support, five allowed unit classes, four evidence cadences, and two eligible construct families: provenance-native facts and measurement-ecosystem meta-measurement.

The charter keeps all original claims descriptive unless a separate causal design is preregistered. It prohibits overall country-worth claims, grades, judgmental labels, traffic-light verdicts, and one overall country rank.

## Novelty and disposition

A candidate must pass both an information test and a preregistered user-task test. It competes with the best coherent single established indicator, a transparent simple aggregate or factor where coherent, and the source-native dashboard/no-score baseline. Cosmetic reweighting, normalization, branding, interface work, or faster updates do not satisfy novelty. The current composite has no incumbency advantage, and no winner is an acceptable outcome.

Published measures face annual review. Two consecutive failures of the same required gate retire the output; serious rights, verifiability, or consequential-harm failures permit immediate suspension.
