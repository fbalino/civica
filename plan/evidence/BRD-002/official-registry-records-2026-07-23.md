## Professional-review queue

Counsel should answer and record:

1. What are the legally relevant launch and use jurisdictions given the
   owner/entity location, website reach, intended audience, DOI/publication,
   APIs/downloads, and any future organizational structure?
2. Does `Civica Atlas` create a likelihood-of-confusion concern with the
   registered UK `CIVICA` word mark or other rights, and does adding `Atlas`
   materially change that analysis?
3. Which Nice classes and exact goods/services describe the current project,
   including public data, APIs, software, downloadable/printed publications,
   research, education, and citation assets?
4. What is the current official status and scope of relevant US, EU, Uruguay,
   WIPO-designated, and common-law rights, including serials 79298217 and
   87939287?
5. How do the project’s non-commercial posture, no-affiliation language, visual
   identity, first-use dates, domain, and intended audience affect the analysis?
6. Are there coexistence, consent, filing, disclaimer, geographic limitation,
   or launch-sequencing options worth considering?
7. Should the owner keep the name, impose interim constraints, or activate the
   reversible rename plan under BRD-006?

The owner must provide factual confirmation of intended geography, legal
entity/owner, first-use evidence, services, commercial plans, and the exact
visual/verbal marks before counsel reviews the packet.
