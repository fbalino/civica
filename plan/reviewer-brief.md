## What Civica Atlas is

**Civica Atlas is a provenance-first comparative reference to how every
country is governed.**

The country atlas is the primary product: country profiles, political
institutions, constitutions, elections, source trails, reconciliation,
versioned exports, and citation support. The intended contribution is an
inspectable comparative reference layer, not a claim to institutional or
academic authority.

## What is experimental

The Civica Index and Civica Pulse are secondary research experiments.

- The Index is a research-beta composite. Its construct, weighting, panel,
  missingness, sensitivity, incremental value, and misuse risk remain under
  evaluation. Current Monte Carlo bounds are input-variation summaries, not
  confidence intervals for a true country score.
- Pulse is being reframed as a provenance-rich governance-event ledger first.
  Model classifications and numeric effects are experimental; model agreement
  is not treated as validation, and no observed event is not evidence of
  stability.

Either experiment may be redesigned, demoted, or retired. A null result is an
acceptable research outcome.

## Claims Civica may make now

- The project publishes a browsable comparative atlas and its current methods.
- Source, vintage, rights, and reconciliation context is available on specified
  surfaces where the underlying records support it.
- Index and Pulse outputs are beta or experimental and have not completed
  independent review.

## Claims Civica does not make now

- That Civica is academically validated, authoritative, or peer-reviewed.
- That every displayed value has complete multi-source provenance.
- That a current Index simulation range locates a latent true score.
- That Pulse is a continuous or validated measure of governance change.
- That advisory-board recruitment or future review implies endorsement.
