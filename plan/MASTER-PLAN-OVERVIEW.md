# Civica Academic Publication Readiness — Owner Overview

## The destination

**Civica Atlas is a provenance-first comparative reference to how every country is governed.**

The atlas is the primary product. Original measurements are secondary research experiments. They can become important, but only after they earn clear constructs, reproducibility, incremental value, uncertainty honesty, misuse resistance, and external review.

The plan does not assume the current Civica Index survives. It also does not assume Civica must abandon original measurement. It creates a fair contest among the current composite, simple established baselines, a source-native evidence dashboard, and more original Civica-native ideas.

## The most promising original ideas

The independent Fable design study identified two especially promising directions:

1. **Measurement Concordance:** show where major governance-measurement projects agree or diverge for a country and construct. This measures the measurement ecosystem, not whether a country is “good.” It must survive source-dependence, midpoint-artifact, stability, and expert-validity tests.
2. **Power and Transfer Ledger:** publish sourced facts about executive tenure, electoral transfers, alternation, and term-limit changes. It is a continuously updated institutional dataset with receipts, not an overall country score. It must survive coding-reliability, historical cross-check, provenance, and freshness tests.

A constitution-to-practice pairing is retained as a slower research track. Pulse becomes a validated governance-event ledger first. The source-native Governance Evidence Dashboard is the floor product even if every original candidate fails.

## What happens in order

### Phase 0 — Truthful beta and control plane

- Correct claims that outrun implementation or data.
- Remove A–F country grades and judgmental bands from the target product.
- Establish the claim tiers, one checklist, evidence rules, CI baseline, and documentation contract.
- Fix release-integrity defects found by the blind audit before they contaminate later analysis.

**Exit:** G1 — public beta language is truthful and every P0 contradiction is owned.

### Phase 1 — Reproducible atlas foundation

- Put every production adapter on canonical `main`.
- Separate observation year, source release, retrieval time, and Civica release.
- Make frozen vintages truly immutable.
- Publish coverage, reconciliation depth, source independence, rights, and known gaps.
- Build the bulk atlas export, codebook, manifests, checksums, clean-room reproduction, and release candidate.
- Deepen source-native trends, elections, constitutional search, and research exports without depending on a Civica score.

**Exit:** G2 — an independently rebuildable atlas release candidate exists.

### Phase 2 — Research tournament and Pulse ledger

Run in parallel:

- **Index lane:** reproduce the incumbent; freeze the full panel; formalize candidates/baselines; preregister thresholds; run longitudinal, out-of-sample, sensitivity, subgroup, novelty, usefulness, and misuse tests; publish a disposition.
- **Pulse lane:** version the ontology/pipeline; preserve evidence; repair clustering and observability; create representative event/no-event samples; prepare double coding; measure retrieval, clustering, attribution, classification, calibration, and subgroup performance; run a prospective shadow period; publish a ledger/numeric-signal disposition.

“No candidate wins” is a successful research outcome. Failed candidates and null results remain archived so they cannot be quietly revived later.

**Exit:** G3 — Index and Pulse each have an evidence-backed disposition.

### Phase 3 — Reader, platform, and verification finish

- Pilot and normalize the engraving color system; regenerate only semantic/art failures.
- Restore the illustration manifest and disclosure.
- Fix the caption structure and create three Explore menu concepts before choosing one.
- Finish design-system, responsive, keyboard, contrast, motion, performance, and visual-regression work.
- Add CI, deterministic lint, API/ingestion contracts, e2e/browser testing, accessibility, performance budgets, job/error monitoring, security, backup/restore, deployment, and rollback evidence.

**Exit:** G4 — all agent-verifiable P0/P1 work is complete. Only explicit human/external checks remain.

### Phase 4 — Human review

- Quietly research qualified reviewers by role; do not contact them early.
- Prepare narrow review packets for atlas/data curation, Index measurement, and Pulse event data.
- Improve the advisory-board route, charter, privacy, and application flow.
- After G4 and owner approval, contact a small ranked set with personalized asks and clear independence/conflict/honorarium terms.
- Preserve reports, publish responses as consented, implement findings, and rerun the release gates.

**Exit:** G5 — independent review is resolved and remaining disagreements are visible.

### Phase 5 — Frozen release and outreach

- Resolve name/trademark/confusion, code/data/art licensing, privacy, terms, accessibility, and noncommercial-source constraints.
- Register the frozen atlas release and DOI.
- Publish reproducible research notes, teaching/notebook assets, sample citations, and a clear methods explainer.
- Run staged outreach to researchers/teachers, research-data librarians, data journalists, governance NGOs, then broader readers.
- Measure citations, downloads, notebook use, teaching adoption, qualified corrections, and reviewer engagement—not only page views.

**Exit:** G6 — the citable release and bounded outreach program are live and monitored.

## What agents can finish before reviewers

Agents can complete nearly all code/data/documentation repair, clean-room reproduction, candidate prototyping, preregistration scaffolding, statistical runs, sensitivity tests, sampling frames, annotation tooling, browser/e2e work, reviewer longlist research, packets, and outreach assets.

Humans are reserved for work that is genuinely human:

- independent construct and codebook judgment;
- blind double coding/adjudication where used as gold evidence;
- constitutional pairing fairness;
- legal advice;
- subjective selection among approved visual concepts;
- final reviewer/contact/name/product decisions.

## The owner's future decisions

No decision is needed to begin the plan. Later gates will bring back only these material choices:

- approve the engraving-grade pilot;
- approve, revise, or reject the large rendered Explore candidate;
- approve a shortlist/contact terms for independent reviewers;
- decide a keep/rename outcome after evidence and professional advice;
- approve the final disposition of Index/Pulse candidates after the declared tests and external review.

## How progress is reported

`plan/MASTER-CHECKLIST.md` is the single execution checklist. Each task also appears in one numbered area file with an objective `Done when`. A task counts only when:

1. its code/data/content change exists;
2. the named tests and browser checks pass;
3. proof is stored under `plan/evidence/<TASK-ID>/`;
4. both checklist copies are checked;
5. `plan/PROGRESS.md` records the result.

Progress reports show checked/total and percentage overall and by area. Manual or external checks never inflate the automated completion percentage.
