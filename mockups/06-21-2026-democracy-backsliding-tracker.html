El Salvador · Pulse profile

From hybrid democracy to one-party rule, in five years.

Civica Index dropped from 62.1 (Jun 2021) to 50.7 (Jun 2026), a five-year delta of −11.4 — the steepest sustained decline in the Americas since Venezuela's 2014–2019 collapse. Decline is concentrated in institutional integrity (judicial independence, term-limit erosion) rather than rights or services.

How "backsliding" is computed

For each country and each Civica Index axis (institutional integrity, rights & freedoms, political accountability), Civica computes a rolling Δ between the latest score and that country's own baseline (mean of the first 24 months of the configurable window — default 5 years). A country is flagged when any axis delta is ≤ −4.0 points and the change is sustained for ≥ 6 consecutive monthly observations (smoothing out single-event noise). Severity bands use the largest of the three axis deltas: severe ≥ 8.0, moderate 4.0 – 7.9, mild 2.0 – 3.9.

Indicator inputs are the same sources already wired into Civica Index v2: V-Dem v15 (live), Freedom House FIW 2026 (live), RSF Press Freedom 2026 (live), plus internal Civica research for non-quantitative events (term-limit compliance, court purges, state-of-exception status). Live vs frozen provenance is rendered with the standard Civica source-dot convention.
