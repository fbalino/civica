What the trajectory tells us

Brazil's democratic indices climbed steadily from 2003 through the 2014 peak, then fell sharply through impeachment, Operation Car Wash, and the 2018–2022 administration. The 2023 transition produced a partial recovery in liberal and electoral components — but the egalitarian and press-freedom scores remain well below their decade highs. Every sub-index now sits beneath the South America regional average.
