Civica Atlas · Mexico · Constitution · Article 83

Single six-year term, no re-election

MX Mexico · Presidential · Adopted 1917 · Last amended 2014

Article 83 — full text

The President shall begin his charge on October 1st and shall last in it six years. The citizen who has occupied the office of President of the Republic, popularly elected, or by the function of law, on no occasion and for no reason whatsoever may again hold that office.
Source: Constitute Project · MX-1917 · clause-id 83.1 · last verified 2026-04-03
