Component Library · Score Display & Embeds

One score.
Two contexts.

The Civica Index score lives in two places: inside our own country pages, and on thousands of sites we’ll never see. Both render from the same tokens, the same tiers, and the same provenance dot. This document is the reference.

Part A · Canonical component

CI / CP score display

Used on every /countries/[slug] profile page, on the compare view, and as the primary hero of the leaderboard row. Two equal-weight halves: the structural index on the left, the live pulse on the right. Provenance dot is non-negotiable.

Part B · Embeddable widget

Civica score embed

Three fixed sizes. A journalist drops a badge into a story; a think tank embeds a card; a country profile on Wikipedia links to the large card. All render the same tokens, always link back to the canonical country page, and display the same provenance dot as our own site.

Part C · Theme variants

Light & dark render identically

The widget inherits the host page’s color scheme via the prefers-color-scheme media query by default, or accepts an explicit &theme=light|dark URL parameter. No JavaScript required on the host side.

Part D · Usage contexts

Where the widget lives

Editorial coverage

Journalists cite live CP in an article about a political event. Small badge inline with the paragraph, linking to the day’s changelog entry.

Size · Small · inline

Think-tank / NGO pages

Country-profile pages on external databases surface the medium card for at-a-glance comparison. Sits inside the stats sidebar.

Size · Medium · sidebar

Government transparency portals

Official pages embed the large card to transparently show their own Civica score with dimension breakdown and live pulse.
