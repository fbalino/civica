RSF Index · five pillars (single CSV, ingested annually)

Political context
Independence of media from political control; pluralism; editorial independence.
Legal framework
Statutory protection for journalism; criminal-libel statutes; censorship laws.
Economic context
Ownership concentration; advertising-market pressure; viability of independent outlets.
Sociocultural context
Social pressure on journalists (gender, religion, ethnicity); cultural taboos.
Safety
Killings, imprisonments, harassment, surveillance — physical and digital threats.

Data: Reporters Without Borders, World Press Freedom Index 2026. Civica ingests one CSV per year and joins it to the existing countries table on ISO-3166 alpha-2. No new schema, no scraping, no scheduled refresh beyond the annual snapshot. Sample scores in this mockup are illustrative; production ingest will use the canonical RSF release.
