What international organizations does France belong to that Germany doesn't?
France belongs to 8 organizations Germany doesn't, most notably:

• Organisation Internationale de la Francophonie (OIF) — France is a founding member; Germany has no official role.
• UN Security Council P5 — France holds a permanent seat; Germany has only served as an elected member.
• Franc Zone (CFA) — France maintains currency ties with 14 African states; Germany has no equivalent.

When did France join NATO?
France was a founding member of NATO in 1949. However, in 1966, President de Gaulle withdrew France from NATO's integrated military command (while remaining a political member). France fully rejoined the military command under President Sarkozy in 2009.
