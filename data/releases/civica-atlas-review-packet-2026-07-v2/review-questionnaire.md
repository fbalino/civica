# Civica Atlas data-curation review questionnaire

**Packet:** civica-atlas-review-packet/2026-07-v2

Please disclose relevant conflicts before review. Cite the artifact used for each answer and assign one finding level: blocking, major, minor, or no concern. A favorable conclusion is not required.

1. Can the frozen release be reproduced from the supplied environment, manifest, codebook, and commands, and do the resulting checksums match?
2. Does the codebook define every released field, unit, null/value state, vintage, derivation, and source meaning needed for independent use?
3. Do the source-input and rights manifests distinguish access, citation, reuse permission, non-commercial restrictions, and excluded payloads accurately?
4. Does the release preserve a sufficiently inspectable provenance chain from each exported fact to source identity and vintage?
5. Are coverage and quality reports scoped honestly, with missing, unknown, unsupported, disputed, stale, and withheld states kept distinct?
6. Are reconciliation, source independence, and precedence rules appropriate for the selected facts and supported by reproducible evidence?
7. Do the known limitations identify the material gaps a researcher or teacher would need before reuse?
8. Are citation metadata, version identity, checksums, and supersession rules adequate for a citable frozen release?
9. Would the correction/retraction process preserve the scholarly record and make material changes discoverable to data users?
10. Which blocking or major change is required before external academic outreach, and which claims should remain narrower even after that change?

Requested output: one bounded written review answering these questions, with evidence references, finding levels, and any unresolved limitation.
