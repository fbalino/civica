# K2 Measurement Concordance prototype v1

K2 compares three institutionally distinct democratic-accountability raters—V-Dem LDI, WGI Voice & Accountability, and Freedom House PR+CL—within their exact common country-year coverage. Each source is converted to a within-year percentile with average tie ranks. The output shows named placements, range, and IQR. It does not average them into country quality.

Institutional separation does not prove evidentiary independence. The publishers can share experts, concepts, sources, and prior ratings. Agreement is not truth; disagreement is not poor governance. Within-source uncertainty is absent from panel v3 and is reported as unavailable rather than folded into rater spread.

Only development-split artifact and source-deletion diagnostics are reported in the checked manifest. Validation and final-holdout decision metrics remain sealed. External contested/consensus known-case validation is pending the preregistered independent expert list.
